## Brand & Style

This design system embodies an unapologetic celebration of high-end skincare and curated beauty rituals, tailored for an intimate, tactile mobile experience. The brand voice is poised, intimate, inspiring, and restorative—treating everyday cosmetic routines as restorative self-care ceremonies. 

The aesthetic marries **Sensory Luxury Minimalism** with **Tactile Feminine Modernism**:
- **Generous Air & Breathing Room**: Content floats within warm ivory canvases, rejecting cluttered retail paradigms in favor of editorial boutique curation.
- **Micro-Delicacy & Soft Structure**: Razor-thin hairline borders in mauve and blush encase tactile white surfaces, providing structure without visual bulk.
- **Lustrous Accentuation**: Vibrant rose pink and deep velvety berry provide decisive interactive feedback, anchored by muted dusty mauve neutrals.
- **Editorial Contrast**: The tension between poetic, high-contrast serif headlines and hyper-legible, geometric sans body typography creates an elevated, couture reading rhythm.

## Colors

The palette leverages a warm, natural skincare undertone rather than sterile digital whites or cold greys.

- **Primary Interactive (`#E85D86`)**: Vibrant Rose Pink. Designated exclusively for primary conversion flows, main call-to-action buttons, active radio selections, and active bag counters. Never used for long-form reading.
- **Secondary Accent (`#F7DCE5`)**: Luminous Blush Pink. Applied to soft pill tags, selected category tiles, notification badges, and tonal progress fills.
- **Tertiary Anchor (`#702D46`)**: Deep Berry. Used for editorial titling accents, luxury product badges, high-priority alert cards, and refined brand icons.
- **Neutral Primary (`#292329`)**: Warm Charcoal. Primary text color, offering softer contrast than absolute black while exceeding WCAG AAA legibility against Ivory.
- **Muted Neutral & Hairlines (`#B99AA8`)**: Soft Mauve. Applied to secondary labels, inactive nav states, timestamps, and 1px borders.
- **Canvas (`#FFF9F8`)**: Soft Ivory. The base canvas for the entire application, imparting warmth and preventing OLED glare.
- **Surfaces (`#FFFFFF` & `#F2ECEA`)**: Pure Warm White is reserved for elevated product cards, modal sheets, and floating bars. Pale Neutral (`#F2ECEA`) is deployed for structural segment controls, search bar fields, and disabled container backgrounds.

## Typography

The type system creates an immediate editorial impression:
- **Serif Editorial Tones (`Playfair Display`)**: Used deliberately for product collection titles, hero headers, marketing promotions, and routine names. Its high stroke contrast reinforces artisanal beauty aesthetics.
- **Modern Performance Sans (`Plus Jakarta Sans`)**: Deployed for UI navigation, descriptions, buttons, ingredients, and transactional states. Its wide apertures and crisp geometry provide effortless scannability on high-density mobile screens.
- **Price Architecture (`PKR`)**: All currency numbers render in `Plus Jakarta Sans` semi-bold/bold with tabular numerals (`tnum`) to maintain structural vertical alignment in cart listings. The currency prefix "PKR" is typeset in `label-sm` or `label-md` with uppercase tracking (+0.06em) adjacent to the numerical value (e.g., `PKR 4,850`).

## Layout & Spacing

The mobile layout system targets Android devices with responsive adaptations for standard and fold-out form factors:

- **Grid & Margins**: A standard 4-column layout on standard phones with `1.25rem` (20px) outer edge margins and a `1rem` (16px) gutter between product card columns. For folded screens and small tablets, the grid automatically adapts to 6 or 8 columns with 24px margins.
- **Tap Targets & Safe Heights**: All interactive targets strictly meet a minimum height of 48dp (Android Material spec). The bottom navigation bar stands at 64dp plus system navigation inset padding.
- **Rhythm**: Component spacing strictly adheres to an 8dp baseline grid (with 4dp micro-increments via `space-xs`). Vertical margins between distinct editorial story sections scale at `space-xl` (36px) to maintain an unhurried, luxury catalogue ambiance.

## Elevation & Depth

Rather than harsh drops or generic grey shadows, this system leverages **Tonal Softness & Tinted Rose Ambiance**:

- **Level 0 (Flat)**: Baseline canvas (`#FFF9F8`). Flat surfaces (input fields, pill filters) merge seamlessly into the canvas or use `#F2ECEA` fill without elevation.
- **Level 1 (Ambient Product Cards)**: Surfaces in `#FFFFFF` stand out with a two-tier rose-tinted shadow:
  - `box-shadow: 0 4px 16px -2px rgba(112, 45, 70, 0.05), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(232, 93, 134, 0.04);`
  - Enclosed with a delicate micro-border: `1px solid rgba(242, 236, 234, 0.8)`.
- **Level 2 (Floating Sheets & Navigation)**: Floating action bars, bottom navigation, and quick-add trays:
  - `box-shadow: 0 10px 30px -4px rgba(112, 45, 70, 0.08), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(41, 35, 41, 0.03);`
  - Frosted blur variant: For top application bars, a 16px blur over a 90% opaque `#FFF9F8` background preserves readability as products scroll beneath.
- **Level 3 (Modals & Dialogs)**: Checkout bottom sheets, drawer navigation, and shade-selector overlays:
  - `box-shadow: 0 20px 40px -8px rgba(112, 45, 70, 0.16);`
  - Backdrop dimming: `rgba(41, 35, 41, 0.45)` with subtle backdrop blur (4px).

## Shapes

The design system employs **Harmonious Rounded Architecture** to evoke beauty packaging (cream jars, glass dropper bottles, and compact cushions):

- **Default Corners**: Structural containers, banners, and product showcase cards use `rounded-2xl` (16px to 24px) for a soft, approachable geometry.
- **Full Pill Geometry (`rounded-full`)**: Applied to all primary CTA buttons, search inputs, active ingredient pills, review rating badges, and promotional tags.
- **Bottom Sheet Morphing**: Modal surfaces descending from or docking to the bottom edge feature top-left and top-right radii of 28px, echoing modern Android sheet surfaces.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary CTA**: Full pill shape (`height: 52px`). Background: `#E85D86`, Text: `#FFFFFF` (`label-lg`), subtle shadow (`rgba(232, 93, 134, 0.25)`). Ripple feedback: Tinted rose wash.
- **Secondary Luxury**: Full pill shape (`height: 52px`). Background: `#FFFFFF`, Border: `1.5px solid #F7DCE5`, Text: `#702D46` (`label-lg`).
- **Tertiary / Ghost**: Borderless, `color: #702D46`, underline animation on focus.

### Chips & Product Tags
- **Filter Pills**: `height: 36px`, full pill radius. Unselected: `#FFFFFF` fill with `1px solid #F2ECEA`, text `#292329`. Selected: `#702D46` fill, text `#FFFFFF`, no border.
- **Ingredient & Skin-Type Badges**: `#F7DCE5` background with `#702D46` text (`label-sm`).
- **Luxury Gold / Prestige Badges**: Soft champagne gradient fill (`#F9F1E7` to `#F3E3D0`) with `#702D46` text and `1px solid rgba(185, 154, 168, 0.3)`.

### Cards & Lists
- **Catalog Product Card**: Background `#FFFFFF`, border-radius 20px, 1px hairline border (`#F2ECEA`). Aspect ratio 1:1.1 for imagery with a subtle soft blush overlay on empty state. Sticky floating heart (wishlist) button top-right in rounded-full `#FFFFFF` with rose tint. Price displayed prominently in PKR.
- **List Items (Routine Steps)**: Horizontal card with rounded 16px frame, showing step index number (`01`, `02`) in `Playfair Display` (`#B99AA8`).

### Input Fields & Controls
- **Search & Text Inputs**: `height: 52px`, rounded-full, background `#F2ECEA`, border `1px solid transparent`. Active/Focus state: background `#FFFFFF`, border `1.5px solid #E85D86`, text `#292329`. Placeholder in `#B99AA8`.
- **Checkboxes & Radios**: 22dp size. Radio: circular with smooth expanding `#E85D86` center dot. Checkbox: rounded-md (6px radius) filled with `#702D46` and crisp white checkmark when checked; border `#B99AA8` when unchecked.

### Navigation & Utilities
- **Bottom Navigation Bar (5 Items)**:
  - Destinations: *Home*, *Categories*, *Wishlist*, *Bag*, *Profile*.
  - Surface: `#FFFFFF` with top micro-border `1px solid #F2ECEA`.
  - Inactive icons: `#B99AA8`, 24dp.
  - Active icon: Filled `#E85D86` with a miniature 4dp dot below the icon.
  - Bag Badge: Micro pill in `#E85D86` with crisp white counter text.
- **Data Sync & Drive State Indicator**: Micro-banner or icon in profile/header signaling local SQLite synchronization with Google Drive backup. State: soft `#F7DCE5` cloud badge with a subtle spinning pulse during active sync, transitioning to a stable checkmark icon.
